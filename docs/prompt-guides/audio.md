# Audio, music and speech prompts

Start with the requested sound and its purpose. Preserve the user's genre,
words, mood and explicit exclusions.

- Music: describe requested genre, instrumentation, tempo or energy, and
  development over the clip. Keep instrumental and vocal directions consistent.
- Sound effects: name the sound source, its action, distance and environment
  when specified. Describe timing or repetition rather than visual camera terms.
- Speech: preserve the exact script and language. Separate delivery direction
  from words to be spoken when the model provides distinct controls.
- Do not invent lyrics, dialogue, a voice identity, or background music.
- A reference may establish timbre or delivery; describe only what is known
  and relevant to the requested change.
- Keep duration and supported engine controls in settings. If a model has
  separate lyrics or style fields, follow the selected profile's mapping.
- Do not promise stereo placement, exact synchronization or seamless looping
  unless the chosen workflow supports it and the user requested it.

Provide a usable prompt and a short explanation in the UI language.
Prompt assistance does not start audio generation.
